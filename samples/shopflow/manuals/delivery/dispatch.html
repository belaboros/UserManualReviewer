2. Dispatch and delivery

2.1 Loading parcels

Parcels heavier than 25 kg must not be loaded onto delivery vans. Return them to packing to be split.

Orders without a postal code cannot be dispatched. The parcel is moved to the Address hold shelf.

  1. Scan the parcel label.
  2. Assign the parcel to a route.
  3. Click Load. The order status changes to Shipped.
  4. Send the ASN to the carrier.

If your site still uses the Classic Dispatch screen, click Dispatch › Classic › Load all instead.

2.2 Delivering to the consignee

  1. When the driver starts the route, the customer receives an "Out for delivery" SMS.
  2. Hand over the parcel and collect a signature on the handheld device.
  3. Click Delivered. The delivery log is written to /var/log/shopflow/delivery.log.

2.3 Failed delivery

If nobody is at home, click Delivery failed. The customer is notified automatically and can choose a new delivery date using the reschedule link in the notification.

After three failed attempts the parcel is returned to the warehouse.

2.4 Planning routes

At the end of each day the dispatcher creates the next day's routes in Dispatch › Route planning. Each route has a van, a driver and a list of postal-code areas.

2.5 Cancelling a shipment

Orders can be cancelled at any time until they reach the status Shipped. To cancel, click Cancel order in the order details.
