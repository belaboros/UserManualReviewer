3. Delivery statuses

StatusMeaning
PaidPayment received; waiting to be released to picking.
PickingAn operator is picking the order.
Ready for dispatchThe parcel is packed and labelled.
ShippedThe parcel is loaded on a van.
Out for deliveryThe driver has started the route.
DeliveredThe consignee signed for the parcel.
Delivery failedDelivery was attempted but not completed.
ReturnedThe parcel is back in the warehouse.

The maximum parcel weight is 30 kg.
