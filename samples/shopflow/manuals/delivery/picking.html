1. Picking and packing

1.1 The picking queue

Orders appear in the Picking queue once they have the status Paid and have been released by the warehouse supervisor. Orders that are not released stay invisible to warehouse operators.

To open the queue, log in to https://shopflow.example.com/wms with your Warehouse role and click Picking.

1.2 Picking an order

  1. Select the order with the highest priority in the queue. Orders close to breaching the picking SLA are shown in red.
  2. Scan each product's barcode with the handheld scanner.
  3. When all line items are scanned, click Picking complete.

1.3 Packing

  1. Choose a box size that fits all items.
  2. Weigh the parcel. The maximum parcel weight is 30 kg.
  3. Print and attach the shipping label.
  4. Click Ready for dispatch.

1.4 Address problems

If the delivery address looks wrong, follow the procedure in the Order Capturing manual, section "Delivery Address Validation".
