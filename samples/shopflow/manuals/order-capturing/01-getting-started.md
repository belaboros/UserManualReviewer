# 1. Getting started

## 1.1 Prerequisites

To capture orders you need:

- A ShopFlow user account with the **Sales Agent** role. Your administrator assigns roles in **Admin › Users › Roles**.
- A supported browser (Chrome 120+, Edge 120+, Firefox 121+).

## 1.2 Logging in

1. Open `https://shopflow.example.com/oms`.
2. Enter your user name and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

The **Orders** dashboard opens.

## 1.3 Adding products to the cart

1. On the **Orders** dashboard, click **New Order**.
2. In the **Product search** field, type a product name or SKU.
3. Select the product from the result list.
4. Enter the quantity and click **Add to cart**.

Repeat these steps for each product. You can add up to **50 line items** to one order.

## 1.4 Personal preferences

To change the interface language, click your name in the top bar and select **Preferences › Language**.

Your session ends after **15 minutes** of inactivity.
