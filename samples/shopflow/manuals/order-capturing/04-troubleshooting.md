# 4. Troubleshooting

## The Place Order button is disabled

The **Place Order** button stays disabled until you accept the terms and conditions.

## I cannot add more products

An order can contain at most **100 line items**. Split larger orders into several orders.

## The interface is in the wrong language

Open **Settings › Language** and choose your language. See also [Logging in](01-getting-started.md#logging-in-to-shopflow).

## I was logged out unexpectedly

Sessions expire after **30 minutes** of inactivity. Log in again; unsaved draft orders are lost.

## The address cannot be validated

Check the street and city spelling. Unvalidated addresses can be submitted, but the delivery team may contact the customer.

## The customer did not receive a confirmation

Confirmation e-mails are sent within 5 minutes. Ask the buyer to check the spam folder.
